Flora Studios / About

Hello, I’m Flora.

I help homeowners see — clearly and beautifully — what their home can become, long before the first wall comes down.

My story

A strong architectural foundation

With over a decade of professional experience in architecture and design, I bring a strong architectural foundation to every project. Seven years of design education — and an MBA — taught me to see a home both as a place to live and as a project that has to come together on time, on budget and on paper.

My approach blends thoughtful aesthetics with the technical understanding needed to create spaces that are both beautiful and buildable. I work closely with you through every step, from layout to finishes, so the result feels cohesive and unmistakably yours.

“Every wall shift, cabinet alignment, and appliance placement is considered with construction in mind — not just aesthetics.”

Today, Flora Studios specializes in residential kitchens, baths and interior space planning — delivering refined floor plans, finish planning and detailed 3D visualizations that bring clarity before construction begins.
